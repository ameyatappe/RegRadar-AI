[SECTION: Purpose]
This policy establishes data retention and archival requirements for all customer and employee records.

[SECTION: Scope]
This policy applies to all departments and third-party processors handling personal data.

[SECTION: Retention Period]
All customer records must be retained for a minimum of 5 years from the date of last interaction.

[SECTION: Archival]
Records older than 3 years must be moved to cold storage within 90 days.

[SECTION: Disposal]
Records past their retention period must be securely deleted within 30 days of expiry.
